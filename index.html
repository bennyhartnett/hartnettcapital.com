01 / The Family Office

Built to think in generations, not quarters.

Hartnett Capital is an independent family office designed around patience, discretion, and direct accountability. We pursue a limited number of opportunities where our capital and perspective can be genuinely useful.

Our permanent capital gives us the freedom to invest across stages, structures, and asset classes without forcing an opportunity into a predetermined mandate. The common thread is simple: clear fundamentals and durable relevance.

02 / Strategy

Where we concentrate attention.

01

Operating Businesses

Profitable or emerging companies with differentiated capabilities, sensible economics, and room for durable, technology-enabled growth.

02

Real & Strategic Assets

Property, infrastructure, and scarce assets supported by practical utility, enduring demand, or a distinct location advantage.

03

Special Situations

Complex, overlooked, or transitional opportunities where speed, creativity, and flexible capital matter more than a conventional process.
